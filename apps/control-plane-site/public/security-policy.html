Keep your workspace safe.

Control Plane gives coding agents access to real projects. Choose trusted environments and protect the credentials that connect you to them.

Protect your connections

  • Treat pairing links, authentication tokens, and API keys as secrets.
  • Connect only to environments you trust and are authorized to access.
  • Use appropriately secured networks and remote access. Keep your operating system and Control Plane current.
  • Review agent actions and maintain backups of important work.

Report a security concern

Email support@lucive.app with “Control Plane security” in the subject. Describe the affected version, the issue, and how to reproduce it without sharing active secrets. Please contact us privately before disclosing details that could put others at risk.
